3D element ideas

Built on the organisers' setup

The organisers supply the walls, table, chairs, power, lights and carpet. Visitors arrive from the left, so the right wall, which faces them, carries Socratic and GAVIS takes the left wall with photos of the real device. The 65" board, in a free-standing Formex cover angled 35° across the back-right corner so it faces visitors arriving from the left and leaves the back wall readable, runs both the handwriting board and the 3D Lab by touch. The organisers' table becomes the welcome counter at the front, with the GAVIS headset on a mannequin head to try on.

Formex cover for the 65" board

Front in 15 mm Formex, CNC cut to the bezel plus 5 mm a side; sides and top in 10 mm Formex joined with aluminium angle. The back stays open so the screen rolls in on its own stand. Toggle "X-ray" on the cover to see the stand inside.

Visual DNA

FireSense: charcoal and ember red, rising plume and embers, "!" mark, amber sensor hexagons. GAVIS: deep violet, sound arcs, gaze rays and a waveform, "G" mark. Socratic: ink blue on graph paper, orange question dot, the hook mark.

Letters on top of the walls

The printed walls are 2 000 mm tall with 3D letters standing on their top edge, so the letters read above visitors' heads and never cover the print. "Text column" on a wall keeps its text clear of the hexagons and seals.

3D Lab station: camera looking down

A webcam on an arm points straight down at a 950 mm hand desk. Visitors stand at the desk, move their hands over it and watch the screen. The camera sees only hands on a dark surface, not the people behind. The green volume is the camera field. Hidden by default: there is no room for it in 3 m.

Why everything faces the aisle

The neighbouring booths have closed walls and few visitors step inside, so only the faces, blade signs and flags are seen. Click “Approach from left” or “Approach from right” to check each direction.

Back-lit 3D letters

Three layers: white face, accent-colour body, glow behind. Mounted on the header.

Question-mark beacon

An upright LED ring with a 3D "?" on top of a totem, turning slowly, right by the aisle.

Raised hexagon cluster

Foam-board hexagon modules raised 30–120 mm, some lit, fixed to the backdrop.

Glass AI core

A clear acrylic case on a plinth with a glowing polyhedron spinning inside.

3D hologram fan

A spinning LED fan that floats an image in mid-air, eye-catching from afar.

Hanging halo ring

A Ø1.3 m LED ring hung over the demo area, lighting the "Stand here" circle on the floor.
